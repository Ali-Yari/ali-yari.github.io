01 / About
Researching the changing relationship between AI, organizations, and decision-making.

I am an Information Systems researcher interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence, organizations, and digital transformation.

My research examines how emerging AI technologies influence organizational processes, decision authority, coordination, and human–AI collaboration.

I am particularly interested in what happens when AI systems move beyond being passive tools and increasingly become autonomous participants in organizational work.
