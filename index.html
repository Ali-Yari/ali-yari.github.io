01 / About

Research at the intersection of AI & organizations.

I am an Information Systems researcher interested in artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and organizational decision-making.

My research explores how emerging AI technologies reshape organizational processes, decision authority, coordination, and human–AI collaboration.

I am particularly interested in the organizational implications of increasingly autonomous AI systems and how organizations can develop effective mechanisms for governing and coordinating them.
