01 — About

Research at the intersection of AI, information systems, and organizations.

My research examines how organizations adapt when AI moves from being a tool to becoming an increasingly autonomous participant in organizational processes.

I hold an M.Sc. in Information Technology Management — Advanced Information Systems from Allameh Tabataba’i University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Information Systems, and decision-making, with a focus on Generative AI, Agentic AI, human–AI collaboration, and AI-enabled business transformation.

I am particularly interested in how organizations adapt their coordination and decision-making processes as AI systems become increasingly autonomous.
